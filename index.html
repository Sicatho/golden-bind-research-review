The publishing rhythm

First: a welcome video and an introduction across the approved platforms.

Then: one owner-led video a day, supported by several distinct short posts. The research bank is a choice pool for the week.

Full cadence & channel mix

  • Daily production target: one owner-led video and four short research topics. The 100-card bank is a choice pool, not a requirement to publish 100 original topics in seven days.

  • X and Threads: four short posts at roughly 10:00, 12:00, 15:00 and 19:00 ET. On launch day the welcome replaces the first short slot; avoid dumping all facts at once.

  • Facebook: two or three feed placements at 10:00, 13:00 and 18:00 ET; the daily video replaces one placement. Instagram: one Reel plus at most one distinct carousel; use two or three optional Story frames for extra facts.

  • YouTube: one video/day as a production goal, not an algorithm requirement. Shorts test 18:00 ET (Friday may test 16:00); longer videos test 10:00 ET, especially Sunday. Prioritize clear sound, readable subtitles and a complete story over filling a slot.

  • LinkedIn: one owner-written post/day at 16:00 ET. Pinterest: two distinct Pins/day at 12:00 and 20:00 ET as test windows; refill its native ten-Pin queue.

  • The main four short topics can be adapted across eligible channels. Owners approve platform wording; Instagram captions should not be treated as clickable website links—use the profile link or an available Story link. Pinterest Pins and link-friendly networks use the verified destination.

  • On Nov 26 keep a lighter optional schedule and avoid making US Thanksgiving sound Canadian. Nov 27 is optional shopping/culture context, not a requirement to invent a sale.

  • After 48 hours and at week-end record reach, meaningful replies, saves, profile visits, link clicks and video retention. Compare rates and topics, not only raw views. No promise of audience growth from frequency alone.

Review, then release. This page does not schedule, upload or publish social posts. Shortlisting an idea is a planning choice.

  • Use YouTube Studio for videos and Meta Business Suite for Facebook/Instagram where the connected account supports the chosen format. Native account access and format support still need a pre-scheduling check.

  • Use native Pinterest business scheduling (ten future Pins) and Threads’ available native scheduling. If a native route is unavailable, leave an explicit manual task; do not assume the Metricool connector removes its plan limits.

Evidence and trend freshness

  • Facts and links were researched Nov 17, 2026. Cards labelled current signal cite a dated exhibition, report or platform forecast; they are not a ranking of today’s most discussed topics.

  • Pinterest’s 2026 forecast uses earlier data. AI governance reports establish a current subject, not proof a specific novel is viral. Evergreen and seasonal cards are labelled separately.

  • Recheck the handful of timely hooks 48 hours before release; if the news changes, keep the historical fact and remove the trend claim.

  • Metricool timing data returned nonzero Facebook/YouTube scores but no explained sample or units, and Instagram was all zeros. These are not treated as Golden Bind audience evidence. The first week is an explicit test. Buffer’s YouTube summary table and detailed section disagree on long-form timing; this plan uses its detailed morning result as a test, not a universal optimum.

Video materials and rights

  • Starter footage is downloaded, not a generated or edited video. Use the manifest’s ready/hold states and exact attribution text. An institutional image link alone is not permission.

  • Prefer CC0 and globally reusable CC BY items. US-only public-domain items stay on hold for US/Canada and worldwide social publication until their broader rights are established.

  • Stock footage is illustrative: label a generic river or ocean accurately, never as the Mississippi, a particular voyage, or a real story location unless provenance proves it.

  • Build a clean owner-voiced video master without a platform watermark; adapt aspect ratio, safe text areas and subtitles after the script. Do not reuse unlicensed film adaptations, music or book-cover art.

  • On-screen Golden Bind captures need a clean session with no account details, private writing or late-chapter spoilers.
